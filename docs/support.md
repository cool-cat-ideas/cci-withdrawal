# Support

## Choose the right support channel

We are happy to help if a step is unclear or something is not working as expected. You can describe the situation in your own words.

For a private question, order or licence issue, use [Services and support](https://coolcatideas.com/en/account/services/) in your Cool Cat Ideas account:

1. Sign in, or create an account if you do not have one.
2. Under **Contact the team**, choose **General question**. If your licence includes active technical support, you can instead choose **Technical support for a licence** and select the relevant **Licence**.
3. Enter a subject and describe the issue. A screenshot and the installed product version can help. Please leave out passwords, licence keys and customer data.
4. Click **Send message**. Your conversation opens after submission, and you can return to it under **Your conversations** on the same page.

You can send a general question without an active support licence. For an installation or technical intervention you already purchased, open the item under **Purchased services** and use its linked conversation.

For an unclear documentation step or a broken link, use **Report an issue** on the [online documentation page](https://coolcatideas.com/en/docs/cci-withdrawal-form/1.0.0/support/). It opens the documentation feedback form. To report a security vulnerability, please [email info@coolcatideas.com](mailto:info@coolcatideas.com) rather than posting the details publicly.

Never include passwords, order access keys, identity documents, personal data or production database exports in a public post.

Describe what you tried and the result. These details help when available;
include what you know, and we can ask for anything else later:

- module, PrestaShop and PHP versions;
- active shop, language and theme;
- whether the order belongs to a customer or guest;
- order state and configured merchandise-return period;
- enabled buyer types and exclusion settings;
- exact reproduction steps and the visible error;
- relevant PrestaShop logs with personal data removed.

Do not send production credentials, full database dumps, email contents or
customer identity documents through an ordinary support channel. Use a staging
copy and temporary least-privilege access when secure access is necessary.

The module works without a license key. Legal classification of a
specific purchase, custom theme work and store-process consulting are separate
services rather than defects in the module package.
